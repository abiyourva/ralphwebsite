# Social Media Context — Ralph Estep Jr.

## Who He Is

Ralph Estep Jr. — Licensed Public Accountant (**LPA, never CPA**), 30+ years in
practice, founder of Saggio Management Group in Middletown, Delaware. Business
coach, author, and podcaster. Runs ralphestepjr.com: accounting/tax how-to
content, business coaching, personal finance, and (new) a "Perspective"
category for personal opinion essays.

**Core mission**: people already have the capacity to figure things out — they
just need someone in their corner and the right tools. Never talks down to
his audience.

**Brand palette**: navy `#1B2D4F`, gold `#C8973D`.

## Audience

Regular people who feel anxious, behind, or ashamed about money, and who have
been talked down to by other financial voices. Segments: existing podcast
listeners (Becoming Financially Confident, Financially Confident Christian,
Truth Unveiled), cold/new audiences discovering him for the first time,
content creators (Content Creator's Accountant audience), faith-oriented
community.

## Voice

**Tone**: warm, not clinical. Confident, not arrogant. Direct, not harsh.
Hopeful, but honest about the hard parts. Plainspoken, grounded, like a
trusted advisor. Short sentences. Concrete examples. Minimal jargon.

**Signature lines** (use and echo these):
- "Not just informed. Equipped."
- "Real tools. Real confidence. Real change."

**His metaphors** (he actually uses these): discipline, the open road, steady
routine (he's a motorcyclist and daily exerciser); man of faith, husband,
proud grandfather ("Pop Pop"); practitioner, not theorist — he's done what he
teaches.

**Faith**: faith-informed, never preachy. Empathetic to financial struggle
without being condescending.

## Hard Rules — Do Not Violate

- **Never call him a CPA.** Always Licensed Public Accountant or LPA.
- **No hashtags or emojis in social copy.** This overrides any platform-default
  hashtag/emoji guidance elsewhere — Ralph's established voice runs caption-free
  of both, across every platform.
- No harsh, tough-love, finger-wagging tone (the opposite of Dave Ramsey style).
- No shame. Zero judgment.
- No jargon without explanation.
- No em dashes (use commas or periods).
- No AI vocabulary: elevate, clarity, landscape, navigate, leverage, delve,
  crucial, actionable, foster, highlight (as a verb), showcase, tapestry,
  underscore, vibrant, enduring.
- No sentences starting with "but," "however," or "therefore."
- No rule-of-three lists or artificial structure.

## Voice by Channel

- **Social & email**: short, punchy, human. One idea per post. Open with a
  hook that names the reader's feeling. Close with one small action or warm
  nudge. Taglines work as sign-offs.
- **Blog / articles**: clear, scannable, genuinely helpful. Practical how-to
  framing for the accounting content; first-person and reflective for
  Perspective essays. Answer real search questions where relevant.
- **Perspective essays** (new category, e.g. "Free Isn't Free"): personal,
  first-person, willing to sit with a hard topic without outrage-bait.
  Concedes the other side's real grievances before making his case. Ties back
  to his 30 years of practice as the credibility anchor.

## CTA Philosophy

Next steps should feel natural, never a hard sell. Lead with free value.
Position paid offerings (discovery calls, coaching, courses) as deepening
tools for people who are ready — not pressure.
